# Dive 11 — The data-archaeology compiler: imputation leakage, the certify-the-mass audit law, and a provenance-typed likelihood (B5 / O1 / O3)

**Item:** B5 — O1/O3, "the data-archaeology compiler": a spend-data taxonomy standard + LLM-assisted extraction/normalization + geo-imputation with propagated uncertainty. **Status of headline results:** the **imputation leakage law** (closed form, exact OVB, sign-flip across the Hill knee), the **"imputation manufactures the bias" ranking** (geo-latent > national > geo-plug-in), the **latent-loading repair** (with adstock profiled) and the **certify-top-k MSE law** are **verified** (derivation + Monte Carlo + independent clean-room re-implementation to ±0.01). The **negative result on random-sample rectification** (PPI/difference estimator and matrix calibration are MSE-worse than the raw LLM labels at any realistic audit budget under Pareto spends) is **verified for the simulated family**. The **non-identification of misallocation vs. effectiveness heterogeneity under Hill** is **demonstrated** (profile-likelihood flatness), not proven. The **compiler architecture** (§3.5: provenance types → likelihood terms; the leak test; the audit ledger) is **design grounded in the theorems**, not a field result.

---

## 1. Problem statement

O1 says 70–80% of an MMM project is finding and cleaning spend data; O3 says the statistically superior geo-hierarchical tier demands geo-level spend that many channels (national TV, sponsorships, some walled gardens) never report, so practitioners impute it by population share. The catalog asks for an operational construction: a taxonomy standard, an LLM extraction pipeline, and geo-imputation whose error enters the MMM likelihood rather than being silently treated as data.

The dive takes the *compiler* view: every spend series that reaches the model carries a **provenance type** — *measured*, *classified* (line items assigned to channels by an LLM with a confusion matrix), or *imputed* (national total split by an assumed share vector) — and the compiler's job is to emit, for each type, (i) the correct likelihood term and (ii) the cheapest data-acquisition action that makes the residual error decision-negligible. Three questions had to be answered mathematically first:

1. **What does population-share imputation do to a geo MMM?** Not just to the imputed channel (Sun et al. 2017 showed positive bias in its response curve) but to the *correctly measured* channels sitting next to it.
2. **What does LLM misclassification of line items do, and how should a finite human-audit budget be spent?** Prediction-powered inference (Angelopoulos et al. 2023) and DSL (Egami et al. 2023) are the modern candidates; do they fit heavy-tailed spend?
3. **Which data defects are worth fixing?** A decision-loss ledger in dive 03/08 units.

Formal setup (Part I). G geos, T weeks, per-capita outcome y_gt = μ_g + β_TV f_TV(a^TV_gt) + β_D f_D(a^D_gt) + season_t + ε_gt. National per-capita paths S_t (TV, bought nationally) and D_t (digital, geo-measured). True TV per-capita spend x^TV_gt = S_t(1+η_g+e_gt): η_g a persistent relative misallocation (DMA CPM differences, coverage, local weight), e_gt time-varying share noise; population-share imputation sets x̂^TV_gt = S_t for every geo. Digital x^D_gt = D_t(1+ζ_g). The advertiser's customers concentrate somewhere, so Corr(η, ζ) = ρ_ηζ ≠ 0 is the realistic case. Geometric adstock; f linear or Hill(K,s). Setup (Part II): N line items with spends s_i (Pareto tail index α, the empirical shape of ad accounts), true channel c_i, LLM label ĉ_i drawn from a confusion matrix M[c,ĉ]; an audit budget of n human labels.

## 2. Prior state

Literature subagent (26 searches; sources §9):

- **The only MMM-specific result is Sun, Wang, Jin, Chan & Koehler (Google 2017) §4.4:** naive population-share imputation gives "larger biases and wider credible intervals for the response curves," biases are *positive*, and things worsen as more channels are imputed. They report only the imputed channels' curves — not what happens to the channels that were measured correctly. Meridian's docs (2026-05) still recommend population-share imputation and cite this section; there is no `national_media` flag with model-side uncertainty in Meridian, PyMC-Marketing, LightweightMMM (national cost only) or Robyn (no geo mode). The Unified MMM Schema can *represent* national-only spend (`geo_info` unset) but the trainer requires an imputed array.
- **Measurement-error theory** (Carroll et al. 2006): population-share allocation is *Berkson* error (truth scatters around the assigned value), harmless for slopes in linear models but not through Hill; Abel (2017) gives the multivariate "weight-shifting" result for classical error in one regressor biasing all coefficients. Nobody has combined the two with a geo panel.
- **Misclassification + validation designs:** Gustafson (2003), Buonaccorsi (2010), Amorim et al. (2021, two-phase designs); monetary-unit/PPS audit sampling (PCAOB AS 2315). **LLM-label correction:** PPI (Angelopoulos et al. 2023, *Science*), PPI++, DSL (Egami et al. 2023), Battaglia et al. (2024) on AI-generated regressors — all built for *means and regression coefficients over i.i.d. units*, not for spend-weighted totals with Pareto weights.
- **Taxonomy:** no channel/line-item spend taxonomy standard exists (IAB taxonomies classify content/audience/product); naming-convention guidance is vendor blog material (Supermetrics, Adverity, Claravine, Funnel's "semantic layer").
- **Confirmed gaps:** (1) no derivation of how imputing a national channel biases the *measured* channels in a geo MMM; (2) no Berkson-through-Hill treatment; (3) no audit-size rule or PPS/two-phase design for LLM-classified spend; (4) no provenance-typed likelihood.

## 3. The constructions

### 3.1 The imputation leakage law (linear response; derived here; exact)

Fitting the plug-in geo model (common β_TV on x̂^TV, geo fixed effects, geo-constant seasonal basis) omits the term z_gt = β_TV·a(S)_t·η_g (the adstocked national path times the geo's misallocation). Because the omitted term is a *national time shape times a geo loading*, geo-constant controls cannot absorb it and geo fixed effects only remove its mean. Projecting z on the fitted design gives, with s_t = a(S)_t − mean, d_t = a(D)_t − mean, r_t = d residualised on the geo-constant columns (imputed TV path + seasonal basis), and unweighted geo moments:

  **bias(b_D) / β_TV = [ Σ_t s_t d_t · Cov_g(η, ζ) ] / [ Σ_t d_t² · Var_g(ζ) + Σ_t r_t² · (1+ζ̄)² ].**

Three consequences. (i) The numerator is the **raw** (not seasonally residualised) time cross-product of the two national paths: **seasonality controls do not protect you**, because the leak runs through geo-heterogeneous seasonal *amplitude* (E1 config 4: leak +0.17 with zero non-seasonal correlation between the paths). (ii) The denominator says the leak is largest when the measured channel's *own* geo-heterogeneity is modest and its time path is mostly shared shape (r small) — the ordinary Q4-ramp world. (iii) The imputed channel's own coefficient is pulled the other way (its variance was handed to digital): baseline scenario TV −7%, digital +17%; strong scenario (ρ_SD = 0.9, ρ_ηζ = 0.8, σ = 0.5) TV −48%, digital +79%. The closed form matches the exact finite-sample OVB to three decimals and the Monte Carlo mean to ±0.01 in every configuration (E1; clean-room ±0.01, per-replication correlation 0.86–0.99).

**Corollary (imputation manufactures the bias).** The population-weighted *national* model on the same data is unbiased for both channels (E13: +0.00/+0.00), because aggregation kills the geo loading. So a geo model with a common coefficient on an imputed channel is **strictly worse than not having geos at all** for the measured channels — it converts the measured channel's geo heterogeneity into bias. Ranking (strong scenario, b_D bias / sd): geo-plug-in +0.79/0.12; national +0.00/0.07; geo-latent (next section) +0.00/0.04.

### 3.2 The latent-loading repair and the leak test

Model the imputed channel with a **geo-specific loading on the national path**: y_gt = μ_g + λ_g·a(S)_t + β_D a(D)_gt + …, β_TV = Σ_g p_g λ_g. This is exactly identified when the national path is not collinear with the measured channels' geo paths, and it is *what a hierarchical geo-varying coefficient already does in the linear case* — with the interpretive twist that the recovered "geo heterogeneity of TV effectiveness" is, in truth, misallocation. E2: bias 0.00 at every ρ_SD from 0 to 0.995, sd rising from 0.024 to 0.058 (oracle 0.023–0.039): the repair costs little precision even under near-collinear paths. E3: **hierarchical shrinkage re-opens the leak** once the prior on geo heterogeneity is tighter than the true misallocation (prior sd 0.5 → bias +0.05; 0.16 → +0.30; 0.05 → +0.68); a Meridian-style estimated heterogeneity scale is therefore protective only if the data are allowed to push it up. E14: geo × season interaction controls remove the purely seasonal leak (ρ_SD = 0: 0.32 → 0.01) but leave half of the correlated-path leak (ρ_SD = 0.9: 0.77 → 0.39) at a precision cost; the latent loading removes all of it at better precision.

**The leak test.** Because the plug-in and the latent-loading fits agree iff Cov_g(η, ζ)·Σ s d ≈ 0, the Hausman-type difference b_D^{plug-in} − b_D^{latent} is a specification test the compiler can run on every imputed channel. E12 (power control): |t| = 4.0 at (G, T) = (20, 52), 5.2 at (50, 104), 7.8 at (100, 156) when the leak exists; 0.0–0.2 when ρ_ηζ = 0.

### 3.3 Hill response: sign flip across the knee, and what is not identified

With f = Hill, the omitted term is β[H(a(1+η)) − H(a)] ≈ β·a_t H'(a_t)·η_g, so the linear law applies with s_t replaced by the time-demeaned **elasticity path** a_t H'(a_t). That path's time variation vanishes where d/da[a H'(a)] = 0 — the knee (a = K for s = 1) — and reverses sign beyond it. E17 (β_TV = 3, K = 1.5): leak into digital +0.11 at a/K = 0.2, +0.09 at 0.8, +0.03 at 1.3, 0.00 at 2.0, **−0.05 at 4.0**; the exact-OVB-at-true-K prediction tracks every point (+0.11, +0.08, +0.04, −0.00, −0.04). The imputed channel's own national mROAS under the plug-in runs from −36% (a/K = 0.2) through −4% (1.3) to +27% (4.0); the hierarchical-β model does much better below the knee (−11%, −6%, +1%) but is itself +25% deep in saturation, and at very large misallocation (σ_η = 0.8) both models overstate (plug-in +24%, hier +52%, E5). This reconciles Sun et al.'s *positive* bias with the *negative* TV bias found here: persistent misallocation attenuates through the leak (below the knee), while **time-varying** share noise is Berkson-through-Hill and biases *upward* (E7: linear unbiased at every σ_e as Berkson theory requires; Hill plug-in +1.5% at σ_e = 0.3, +21% at 0.6).

**Non-identification (demonstrated, E19).** With common (β, K) and geo loadings η_g inside the Hill argument, the profiled SSR is flat in K to 0.6% across K ∈ [0.5, 3] and the recovered η̂ has correlation −0.27 to +0.55 with the truth: near the knee, the *amplitude* of a geo's response to national fluctuations is stationary in η, so **misallocation and effectiveness heterogeneity are observationally equivalent to first order and imply different marginal ROI**. The linear repair works because amplitude ∝ (1+η); the Hill repair fails precisely where the leak is small. The compiler must therefore record the imputed channel's national curve as *bracketed*, not estimated, unless geo spend is obtained.

### 3.4 Taxonomy misclassification: the certify-the-mass law

Channel total error under LLM labels is Δ_k = Σ_i s_i(1[ĉ_i = k] − 1[c_i = k]); certifying (human-labelling) a set A zeroes its terms. For a confusion matrix with off-diagonal rate e, the residual after certifying the k largest lines has

  **Var ≈ e(1−e) Σ_{i∉A} s_i² (over in- and out-flow classes),  Bias = e·(Σ_{i∉A, inflow} s_i − Σ_{i∉A, outflow} s_i),**

verified within 1–5% at every k ≥ 20 (E16; clean-room 1–3%). Two structural facts follow. (a) Under Pareto α < 2, Σ s² is dominated by the top lines (α = 1.3: 90% of Σ s² in 0.2% of lines, 99% in 12.6%; E8), so **certifying 2.5% of lines by spend cuts channel-total RMSE from ~5% to 1.5% and the flow bias from 2.2% to 0.3%** (E9); with only 60 lines a week, certifying the top 3 takes a national MMM coefficient from −6% bias / 12% RMSE to 0% / 1.3% (E18). At k = 0 the RMSE is *itself unstable across seeds* (clean-room: 0.165 vs our 0.05) because a single mislabelled top line can move the total — which is the strongest argument for certification. (b) **Random-sample rectification is the wrong tool.** The PPI/difference estimator adds variance (N/n − 1)·V_tail: at n = 400 of N = 2000 (20% of lines labelled) it is still 2× worse than the raw LLM labels (RMSE 0.10 vs 0.05; E9), PPS sampling 0.04, and confusion-matrix calibration (n labels → M̂ → M̂^{−T}) 0.07 — because a 10% error rate estimated from ~130 labels per class carries 2.6 points of rate uncertainty times the whole tail spend. PPI's guarantee is validity against the *n-label classical estimator*, not MSE against the raw predictions, and for spend totals the raw predictions are already within a few percent. Robustness: the ranking is unchanged across α ∈ {1.3, 2, 3}, size-dependent error (large lines labelled better) and asymmetric flow matrices; with errors clustered by campaign family (a naming pattern misread wholesale), certifying by **family mass** beats certifying by line (E15: 0.013 vs 0.017 at n = 50; 0.000 vs 0.009 at n = 200).

**Audit rule.** Sort by (family) spend; certify down the list until Σ_{tail} s_i² e(1−e) + [e·net-flow_tail]² < (τ·T_k)² for the tolerance τ the decision needs (dive 08's deadband gives τ); use a *small* random tail sample only to estimate e for the formula, never to rectify totals. The first-order rule, from the ledger below: τ ≈ 3% is decision-free.

### 3.5 The compiler (design)

**Taxonomy standard** (minimal, machine-checkable): every line item carries {channel, tactic, buying model, geo scope ∈ {geo-measured, national, unknown}, funnel/objective, spend basis (gross/net/fee-inclusive), currency, date range, source system, **provenance type** ∈ {measured, classified(e, family-id, certified?), imputed(share-vector, share-uncertainty class)}}. Extraction is an LLM mapping from raw campaign strings to this schema with a per-line confidence and a family-id (shared naming pattern), which is what the audit law needs.

**Provenance → likelihood term.** *Measured*: ordinary. *Classified*: after certification to tolerance τ, ordinary; the residual variance is an inflation of the channel-spend's measurement covariance (classical, small). *Imputed, persistent misallocation*: geo-specific loading λ_g on the national path with an **un-shrunk** (or data-scaled) heterogeneity prior; report β_TV = Σ p_g λ_g; run the leak test; label the channel's national response curve *bracketed* under saturation. *Imputed, time-varying share noise*: Berkson term — E[H(a(1+e))] ≈ H + ½H''a²σ_e², Var += β²H'²a²σ_e² — added to the likelihood (dive 06's Jensen algebra), which is where "propagated imputation uncertainty" actually belongs. *Imputed with auxiliary geo signal* (impressions, GRPs, site traffic): use it as the share vector and reclassify to *measured-with-noise*.

**Archaeology ledger** (E10, two-channel Hill allocation, regret in % of optimal revenue, se ≤ 0.05): clean data 0.01%; imputation leak, baseline (TV −7%, D +17%) **0.28%**; strong (TV −48%, D +79%) **8.2%**; taxonomy raw LLM (−6% ± 12%) 0.13%; taxonomy after certifying the top 5% of lines 0.00%; Hill Berkson share noise σ_e = 0.6 (TV +21%) 0.20%. Reading: for a $100M budget the baseline leak is worth ~$280k/yr of regret and the strong case ~$8M — geo spend for the imputed channel (or the latent-loading fit, which is free) is the highest-value archaeology; line-item certification is cheap and closes its gap completely; time-varying share noise matters only if it is large.

## 4. Attack-and-refine history

**Round 1 (construct).** Leakage law with the paths *residualised on the seasonal basis*; latent-loading repair; PPI rectification as the taxonomy fix; ledger.

**Round 2 (attack).** (a) The first closed form under-predicted the leak by 4–6× and failed the ρ_SD = 0 case (+0.17 measured vs +0.004 predicted): the omitted term's seasonal amplitude is geo-specific, so residualising on geo-constant seasonality was wrong — the correct law uses raw cross-products in the numerator and the residual only in the denominator (§3.1). (b) "The leak is an artefact of missing geo × season controls" — E14: partially true (kills the pure-seasonal leak), but half the correlated-path leak survives. (c) "The repair needs the imputed channel's adstock" — E11: with the wrong α the latent model leaks again (+0.57 at α_fit = 0.7, −0.18 at 0.3). (d) "Extend the repair to Hill" — E19: not identified near the knee. (e) PPI turned out MSE-worse than doing nothing (E9); matrix calibration too. (f) Sun et al. report *positive* bias; we found *negative* TV bias — contradiction or different mechanism? (g) Ledger magnitudes were arbitrary.

**Round 3 (refine).** (a) Re-derived law verified to 3 decimals against exact OVB and clean-room. (c) Profiling α over a grid inside the latent model picks the true α 30/30 and restores bias 0.00 (E20) — the loadings absorb any *shape*, so the likelihood prefers the true adstock. (d) Replaced "repair" with a bracket + the honest non-identification statement, and the hierarchical-β model as the practical Hill choice below the knee. (e) Replaced PPI with the certify-the-mass law and its MSE formula (E16), then attacked it with size-dependent error, α ∈ {1.3, 2, 3}, and family-clustered errors (E15) — survived, with family-mass certification as the clustered variant. (f) Separated persistent misallocation (attenuating, leaking) from time-varying share noise (Berkson-through-Hill, upward, E7) — both mechanisms are real and the sign tells you which dominates. (g) Ledger recomputed from measured biases.

**Round 4 (attack again).** Seeds/sizes (E12; clean-room used different seeds), operating-point sweep (E17), shrinkage sweep (E3), k = 0 instability flagged by the clean-room agent (accepted and reported). No material revision → stop.

**Devil's advocate.** *Leakage law:* the DGP puts all of TV's geo variation in a single persistent loading; real misallocation drifts (BL-new below) and the geo panel may carry geo-varying seasonality of the *outcome* that correlates with η — the law then has extra terms, and the leak test remains the honest instrument. *Manufactured bias:* the national model is unbiased only because there is no unmodeled national confounder; with one, the geo model's advantage returns — for measured channels. *Repair:* λ_g are unshrunk here; a hierarchical scale estimated from 20 geos × 52 weeks may not open wide enough (E3/E12), and under Hill nothing repairs. *Certify-the-mass:* assumes a human can certify a top line with certainty and that errors are independent below the certified set; a systematic error in *how* the LLM reads a whole tactic (e.g., all "CTV" lines to "TV") is a family error — E15's answer holds only if families are identifiable from names. *PPI verdict:* is about MSE of spend totals under Pareto weights; PPI's CI validity is untouched and matters if the org needs an interval on the total rather than a small error. *Ledger:* two channels, one Hill shape, one budget; magnitudes are illustrative, ordering is the claim.

## 6. Limitations and failure modes

Linear-response leakage law only (Hill handled by local elasticity + numerics); two channels (n-channel version is the same projection with a matrix numerator); persistent η with iid time noise (no drifting misallocation, no η correlated with geo seasonality of the outcome); Fourier seasonality rather than Meridian's knot-spline time effects (E14 suggests smoother controls leak *more*); adstock of the measured channel assumed known; audit model assumes certification is exact and Pareto-shaped spends with independent or family-clustered errors; no multiple-imputation congeniality analysis (Meng 1994) — the Berkson term is the congenial replacement. The LLM extraction itself was not simulated beyond a confusion matrix; extraction of *spend basis* (gross vs net vs fee-inclusive) errors are scale errors that this dive does not treat.

## 7. What is established vs. derived vs. speculative

- *Established (prior art):* Berkson error is slope-unbiased in linear models (Carroll et al.); Sun et al.'s positive imputation bias; PPI's validity claim; multivariate weight-shifting (Abel).
- *Derived and verified here:* the leakage law and its raw-cross-product numerator; the manufactured-bias ranking; latent-loading repair with profiled adstock; leak test; Hill sign flip via the elasticity path; certify-top-k MSE law; PPI/matrix-calibration negative result under Pareto weights; family-mass variant; ledger ordering.
- *Demonstrated, not proven:* Hill non-identification near the knee; reconciliation of positive (time-varying) vs negative (persistent) imputation bias.
- *Speculative / design:* the taxonomy standard fields; provenance → likelihood mapping as a product; the audit tolerance τ ≈ 3%.

## 8. Next steps for a future session

1. **n-channel leakage matrix:** generalise §3.1 to k measured channels (numerator becomes Σ_t s_t d_t^{(j)} Cov_g(η, ζ_j), denominator the measured channels' geo-heterogeneity Gram matrix) and test against Meridian's actual knot-spline time effects and geo population scaling.
2. **Drifting misallocation η_gt (AR(1)):** where between the persistent (attenuating) and iid (Berkson-upward) limits does the leak sign change, and does a random-walk loading (state-space λ_gt) repair it? Links dive 07's refresh-flip machinery: adding geos to a refresh may *create* flips through the leak.
3. **Auxiliary-share instruments:** use geo impressions/GRPs/site traffic as a noisy share vector; derive when a noisy measured share beats the latent loading (classical error in the share vs. Berkson in the imputation).
4. **Hill bracket:** compute the identified *set* for the national mROAS of an imputed channel under Hill as the η/β_g exchange curve; deliver it as an interval in dive 08's PoR output.
5. **Field pilot of the leak test:** on any real geo MMM with a nationally-bought channel, fit plug-in vs latent-loading and report the Hausman t; a positive result would be the first field measurement of imputation leakage.
6. **Audit under scale errors:** extend the certify-the-mass law to gross/net/fee-basis misreads (multiplicative, correlated within source system).
