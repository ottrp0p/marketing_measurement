# Resource Library — MMM Adoption Barriers

Sources behind `03_mmm_adoption_barriers.md`. Vendor sources (Recast, Sellforte, Haus, Mutinex, Measured, MASS Analytics, PyMC Labs) have commercial interests; platform-sponsored surveys (Kantar/Meta, HBR/Google) likewise — corroboration noted where it exists.

## Adoption surveys & industry data

1. **Understanding MMM Adoption** — Kantar & Meta (2025, n=1,935). Best available adoption survey: 34% prioritize MMM, one-third don't use it, 31% in-house, 55% conflicting-results, avg 3.8 measurement tools. PDF: https://s3.amazonaws.com/media.mediapost.com/uploads/Kantar___META_Thought_Leadership.pdf
2. **Bridging the MMM Actionability Gap** — HBR Analytic Services / Google (2026). The defining 87%-use / 28%-act gap; 22% leaders vs 42% laggards (Google-sponsored). https://hbr.org/sponsored/2026/03/bridging-the-marketing-mix-modeling-actionability-gap
3. **Marketers embrace MMM but organizational barriers limit impact** — EMARKETER (2026). Independent readout: 46% slow processes, 45% expertise, 41% silos. https://www.emarketer.com/content/marketers-embrace-mmm-roi-demands-mount-organizational-barriers-limit-impact
4. **Nearly half of US marketers plan to invest in MMM** — EMARKETER/TransUnion (2025). 46.9% investment intent; 27.6% rate MMM most dependable. https://www.emarketer.com/content/nearly-half-of-us-marketers-plan-invest-mmm-over-next-year
5. **FAQ on incrementality** — EMARKETER (2026). 52% incrementality adoption; Google's $100K→$5K test-cost drop. https://www.emarketer.com/content/faq-on-incrementality-how-prove-your-ads-actually-work-2026
6. **Media Mix Modeling Trends 2026** — EMARKETER (2026). Data fragmentation/governance as the binding constraint. https://www.emarketer.com/content/media-mix-modeling-trends-2026
7. **The Last Days of Last-Click** — EMARKETER & Snap (2024, n=282). 78.4% still use last-click; 21.5% believe it; 53% say leadership is its biggest believer. PDF: https://www.emarketer.com/uploads/pdf/EMARKETER_SNAP_The_Last_Days_of_Last-Click.pdf
8. **IAB State of Data 2026** — via The Drum (2026). 60–75% say all leading methods underperform on rigor/timeliness/trust. https://www.thedrum.com/news/ai-is-speeding-up-ad-measurement-trust-still-hasn-t-caught-up
9. **Modernizing MMM: Best Practices for Marketers** — IAB (Dec 2025). Industry-body consensus: data governance as prerequisite; refresh cadence; calibration reconciliation. PDF: https://www.iab.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/IAB_Modernizing_MMM_Best_Practices_for_Marketers_December_2025.pdf
10. **Gartner 2025 CMO Spend Survey** — Gartner (2025). Budget austerity context: marketing flatlined at 7.7% of revenue. https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2025-05-12-gartner-2025-cmo-spend-survey-reveals-marketing-budgets-have-flatlined-at-seven-percent-of-overall-company-revenue
11. **ANA: Difficulty Overcoming Organizational Impediments** — ANA (2006). Proof the organizational barriers are 20 years old and method-independent. https://www.ana.net/content/show/id/401
12. **Nielsen 2023 Annual Marketing Report** — Nielsen (2023). 54% confident measuring digital ROI. https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2023/need-for-consistent-measurement-2023-nielsen-annual-marketing-report/

## Mathematical-barrier sources

33. **Two Key Problems that Ail Bayesian MMM** — Aryma Labs. The frequentist-camp attack; the MMM-wars fault line. https://arymalabs.substack.com/p/two-key-problems-that-ails-bayesian · companion: https://arymalabs.substack.com/p/which-technique-provides-for-greater
