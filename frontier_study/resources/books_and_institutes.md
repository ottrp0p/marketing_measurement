# Resource Library — Books, Institutes, and Ongoing Programs

## Core bookshelf

- **Trustworthy Online Controlled Experiments** — Kohavi, Tang & Xu (2020). The A/B testing engineering bible.
- **How Brands Grow** — Byron Sharp (2010) and **How Brands Grow Part 2** — Romaniuk & Sharp (rev. 2021). The empirical laws of brand growth.
- **Better Brand Health** — Jenni Romaniuk (2023). CEP-based brand measurement playbook.
- **The Long and the Short of It** — Binet & Field (IPA, 2013) and **Effectiveness in Context** (2018). Long/short-term effects framework.
- **The Attention Economy and How Media Works** — Karen Nelson-Field (Springer, 2020). Attention measurement foundations.
- **Lemon** (2019) and **Look out** (2021) — Orlando Wood (IPA). Creative effectiveness and attention.
- **Causal Inference: The Mixtape** — Scott Cunningham (free online: https://mixtape.scunning.com) and **The Effect** — Nick Huntington-Klein (free online: https://theeffectbook.net). Best modern applied causal-inference texts.
- **Mostly Harmless Econometrics** — Angrist & Pischke (2009). The classic identification-first econometrics text.
- **Causality** — Judea Pearl (2009) and **Elements of Causal Inference** — Peters, Janzing & Schölkopf (free PDF from MIT Press). The structural/graphical foundations, including transportability.
- **Identification for Prediction and Decision** — Charles Manski (2007). Partial identification — the honest fallback when ad effects aren't point-identified.

## Institutes, programs, and recurring publication venues

- **Ehrenberg-Bass Institute** — https://marketingscience.info — The empirical-generalizations program; corporate sponsors get the full research library.
- **IPA Effectiveness Databank & EffWorks** — https://ipa.co.uk/knowledge/effectiveness-research-analysis — Binet & Field's data source; annual EffWorks conference papers.
- **Thinkbox research** — https://www.thinkbox.tv/research — Profit Ability 2 and the UK econometric syntheses.
- **ARF (Advertising Research Foundation)** — https://thearf.org — Attention validation programs, measurement standards.
- **LinkedIn B2B Institute** — https://business.linkedin.com/marketing-solutions/b2b-institute — 95-5 rule, B2B brand research.
- **MSI (Marketing Science Institute)** — https://www.msi.org — Working papers bridging academia and practice.
- **Google Research (Ads/Measurement)** — https://research.google/pubs/ (filter: advertising) — The MMM/geo-experiment paper lineage.
- **Amazon Science (advertising)** — https://www.amazon.science — AuctionGym, budget-split work.
- **Meta Research / Marketing Science** — https://research.facebook.com — Lift, GeoLift, Robyn, private measurement.
- **NBER Digitization & Marketing working papers** — https://www.nber.org — Where the big ad-effectiveness experiments land first.
- **Key journals**: Marketing Science, Journal of Marketing Research, Quantitative Marketing & Economics, Management Science; plus KDD/AdKDD/WWW/PoPETs for the systems side.
- **arXiv feeds worth watching**: stat.ME + econ.EM (causal panels, experiment design), cs.GT (auctions), cs.CY/cs.CL (LLM simulation of humans).
